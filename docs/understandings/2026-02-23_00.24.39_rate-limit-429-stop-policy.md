# 2026-02-23_00.24.39 - Rate-limit 429 stop policy

- `worker_loop` previously treated all non-timeout/schema subprocess failures as retryable `RuntimeError`, so a `429` could be retried repeatedly.
- The safe behavior is to classify `429`/rate-limit stderr tails as terminal on first hit, mark that task `error`, and stop the worker immediately.
- `process` should propagate that stop condition across worker threads so no new tasks are claimed after a rate-limit hit; queued tasks are intentionally left queued.
